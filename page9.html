The 10th Haneda Aviation Museum Exhibition: "Rediscovering Haneda of Yesteryear"

Look back on the history of Haneda Airport with these valuable exhibits!

 

An exhibition by the NPO Haneda Aerospace Science Museum Promotion Council (HASM), which has been working for many years with the goal of "bringing an aerospace science museum to Haneda," is being held from September 5th (Saturday).

 

This year's theme commemorates the 95th anniversary of the opening of Haneda Airport and the 100th anniversary of the Asahi Shimbun's Aviation Department, which laid the foundation for civil aviation.
Three airport events will also be held during the event.
